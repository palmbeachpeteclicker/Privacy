Common questions

  • Progress lost? Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app removes saved data.
  • Ads not loading? Check your internet connection and try again later. Ad availability varies by region.
  • Privacy or ad choices? See our Privacy Policy. In the app, open Agency → Legal for privacy options where available.
  • Real money? Ad Coins uses virtual currency only. There are no in-app purchases and no cash-out.

Response time

We aim to reply within a few business days. App Store reviews are not monitored for support requests — please use email.
