Privacy Policy

Ad Coins (bundle ID: com.mason.Watch-Ads-for-Coins)
Effective date: August 22, 2026
Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ad Coins (“the App”), published by the Ad Coins developer (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use the App on iOS.

Summary

  • We do not create accounts or ask for your name, email, or phone number inside the App.
  • Game progress is stored on your device.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device and advertising data under Google’s policies.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information we store on your device

The App saves gameplay progress locally on your iPhone or iPad (for example coin balance, upgrades, prestige progress, casino chips, loot, achievements, and settings). This data stays on your device unless you delete the App or clear its data. We do not operate a game server that stores your progress.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads and optional banner ads. Google and its partners may collect and process information such as:

  • Device identifiers and advertising identifiers (subject to your device settings and consent choices)
  • IP address and general location derived from IP
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, whether a rewarded ad was completed)

This information is used to deliver, measure, and improve ads. How Google uses data is described in Google’s own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob & privacy

Where required by law (for example in the EEA/UK), the App will present a consent experience before personalized ads are requested. You can also limit ad tracking in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising / tracking controls on your device).

Apple privacy technologies

The App may use Apple’s advertising attribution technologies (such as SKAdNetwork) so ad networks can measure installs in a privacy-preserving way. We do not use this to identify you personally.

Analytics and crash reporting

The App does not currently include a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK beyond what Apple and Google AdMob may collect as part of their platforms.

Children’s privacy

The App is a satire game about watching ads and includes simulated casino gambling with virtual currency only. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you reset progress in the App or delete the App. Advertising partners retain data according to their own policies. Because we do not host player accounts, deleting the App removes the progress we store on-device.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete local progress in the App / by deleting the App
  • Disable optional banner ads in the App settings (where available)
  • Manage tracking and advertising preferences in iOS Settings
  • Use regional consent controls when shown

International users

Ad partners may process data in the United States or other countries. If you use the App from the EEA, UK, or similar regions, applicable consent and transparency requirements for advertising will apply.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
